My work brings together family constellation and Internal Family Systems. Family constellation reveals the larger system we belong to: the patterns, loyalties and unspoken stories passed down through generations. IFS turns gently inward, helping us meet the different parts of ourselves with curiosity and compassion. Together, they let us tend both the roots and the inner landscape, so real change can grow from the inside out, rippling outward to the whole family.

Family constellation is a therapeutic approach developed by German psychotherapist Bert Hellinger. It sees each person as part of a larger family system, where unresolved trauma, losses, exclusions, or unspoken burdens from earlier generations can be carried unconsciously by those who come after. In a session, participants or objects stand in for family members, and their placement and felt sensations reveal hidden dynamics. The facilitator then helps restore what Hellinger called the “orders of love”: belonging, balance, and each person in their rightful place. Constellations can be done in groups, one-on-one, and in organizations.

IFS was developed by American therapist Richard Schwartz. It views the mind as made up of many “parts,” each with its own feelings and intentions. Some parts protect us, like the inner critic or the people-pleaser, while others, called exiles, carry old pain and wounds. Beneath them all is the Self: a calm, compassionate, curious core that is never damaged. Protectors are thanked and invited to relax, and exiles are gently witnessed and healed.

Integration

You leave with a new inner image. Change continues to unfold in the weeks after the session.

About me

I’m Boutaina Rosen, family constellation facilitator.

For many years I worked with children with special needs, and I noticed that some children changed much faster than others, often when their parents were doing their own inner work. I like to say a child is like a tree: we can keep trimming the branches, but if the soil lacks nourishment, the tree can never fully flourish. The parents and the family system are that soil.

This understanding led me to family constellation. My first constellation on myself was so powerful that I chose to deepen my training before guiding others, so that I could offer this work with integrity and care.

Today I offer one-on-one inner work sessions and group retreats. Together we explore family patterns and the inner parts of you that carry them, gently releasing what no longer serves so you and your loved ones can grow into your full potential.

Questions

Before you book

What is the difference between family constellation and IFS?

Family constellation looks at your family system and what was passed down through generations. IFS looks at your inner world: the parts of you that carry those patterns, and the calm Self beneath them. Together they work from both sides.

Do I need to bring family members?

No. You come alone. Family members are represented by other participants in group retreats, or by objects and floor markers in one-on-one and online sessions.

Does family constellation work online?

Yes. Online sessions use objects or floor markers in your own space, guided step by step over video. Many people find it just as powerful.

I am the parent of a child with special needs. Can this help?

Yes, this is one of my specializations. When parents do their own inner work, the whole family system changes, and children often respond to that.

Do you offer group retreats and work with organizations?

Yes. I run group retreats where participants stand in for each other’s family members, and organizational constellations for teams and workplaces. [Add upcoming retreat dates and location]

Is this a replacement for therapy?

No. Family constellation and inner work support personal growth and can complement therapy, but they are not psychotherapy and don’t replace medical or psychological treatment.
